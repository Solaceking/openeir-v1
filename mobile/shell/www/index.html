Permissions, plainly

OpenEir asks once and says why. Skip anything — the app still works, and you can change this later in Settings → App permissions.

CameraScan the pairing QR code and photograph documents straight into OpenEir.
LocationOnly with SOS: an emergency alert attaches your location so contacts know where you are.
NotificationsMedication reminders and SOS alerts, even when the app is closed.
